# Brent Valley Golf Club — 19 Apr 2026

**Score:** 103 (+3 vs 100)  
**Rating:** ★★★★★

## My Notes

One of my best round of golf yet. Scored 103, including 50 on the back 9 (technically a par on the 100 scale). My putting was really good (really starting to love putting with my SIK mallet putter, hit some really nice putts, with confidence, the DLT tech works and is starting to really improve my putting). Had some very good approach shots too, landing on the green a few times and I'm also pitching a lot better, getting more confidence in my picthing wedge on those short approach shots when in the past I would have relied on toed down chipping technique. Manage to hit some good shots on the par 3s too, including on the 13 and its intimidating gap and tree right in front of the tee box (scored a +1).  Only made one par, on the 8, but it was a good up and down after the tee shot landed in some rough and had to hit between two trees. But a clean approach and a great putt sealed the par. The back 9 was by far the most impressive in consistency, as I did not have any blow out hole nor score any pars or less, but simply kepts my scores within + or - 1 of the expected scoring of each hole, including htting the expected score on 4 of those 9 holes.

I could have probably broke 100 for the first time today if only I had managed to hit my long tee shots more consistently. I tried to stick to my 4 and 5i only to limit slices, and that mostly worked, however I somehow chunked many of my tee shots, ending up straight but not very far, causing me to play catch up on these holes, which cost me points. 

Next step: a range session focused specifically on the 4i to rediscover that swing, and get some reps in with the hybrid too — when I'm hitting it well it feels great and could become a real weapon off the tee on tighter holes.

Overall a really encouraging round. Breaking 100 is close.
